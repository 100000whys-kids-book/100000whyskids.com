THE SKY

True or false?

Why is the sky blue?

The sky is blue because it reflects the ocean.

(Read on to find out!)

The answer

Sunlight scatters, and blue scatters the most!

Sunlight is made of all colors mixed together. When it hits the air, the blue light bounces around the sky more than the others, so blue is what fills your eyes when you look up.

WOW!

On the Moon there’s no air to scatter light, so the sky there looks black even in daytime!

Good questions, answered

Still curious? The full product details are on the book's Amazon page.

?Who is this book for?

Curious kids ages 5 to 10. A 5-year-old can read or listen to the bold answer line on each page, while older kids read on for the full explanation and the WOW! fact. It also works as a read-aloud for younger children.

?Is every page in colour?

Yes. All 130 pages are printed in full colour, and each of the 104 questions has its own illustrated page.

?Paperback, hardcover or Kindle: which should I choose?

The paperback ($19.90) is a big 8.5 × 11 in book. The hardcover ($36.90) has a sturdy laminated hard cover, a lovely choice for a gift. The Kindle edition (from $6.90) arrives instantly; its full-colour pages look best on a colour screen such as a tablet or the Kindle app.

?How is each page organised?

Every page follows the same path: a True or False? statement to test, the answer in one bold line, a short explanation of the why, and a WOW! fact. At the end of each world, a Brain Zone busts three myths and asks four quiz questions, with the answers at the bottom of the page.

?Is there a Book 2?

100,000 Whys for Kids is Book 1 of the Curious Kids Collection, and its last page gives a first peek at Book 2. Grown-ups can grab the free Myth-Buster Cards and be among the first to hear when it's out.
